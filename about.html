About New Community Organization

Founded to bring people together, create opportunities, and promote sustainable livelihoods for women, youth, and farming communities.

Mission

Why we exist

To empower women and youth through skills development, sustainable livelihoods, entrepreneurship, agriculture, and community development initiatives that improve their social and economic well-being.

Vision

Where we're headed

Empowered, self-reliant, and resilient communities where women and youth have opportunities to develop their potential and contribute to sustainable development.

Our Goals

What we're working toward

01

Empower women economically and socially.

02

Empower young people with skills and opportunities.

03

Promote climate-smart agriculture and sustainable livelihoods.

04

Promote entrepreneurship and agribusiness.

05

Support vulnerable people and communities through community development initiatives.

06

Build partnerships that contribute to sustainable community development.

WC

Founder's Story

Wisdom Chinsinde

New Community Organization was founded by Wisdom Chinsinde, who is finishing a Bachelor of Science in Agriculture Extension and pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Management. He also holds a Certificate in Voice of Prophecy and serves as President of CBDA Training.

His passion for community development — particularly the empowerment of women and youth — inspired him to establish an organization that brings people together, creates opportunities, and promotes sustainable livelihoods across Lilongwe District and beyond.
