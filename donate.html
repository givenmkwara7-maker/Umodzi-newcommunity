Where it goes

Every contribution counts

We welcome financial support, grants, donations, equipment, technical assistance, partnerships, and volunteers to support our women and youth empowerment programs.

🍃

Women's programs — training materials, savings-group starter funds, and mentorship.

🌾

Agriculture — seed, tools, and demonstration-plot upkeep for climate-smart farming.

💬

Youth programs — leadership training and mentorship resources.

🏠

Community outreach — event logistics and community mobilization.

Prefer to give equipment, technical assistance, or a partnership instead of cash? Use the form and select "Other support" — our team will follow up to arrange details.
